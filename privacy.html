Tomorrow Starts Tonight

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Tomorrow Starts Tonight ("we", "us") is a planning app at tomorrowstartstonight.co.uk. We are the controller of the personal information described here. Questions or requests: hello@tomorrowstartstonight.co.uk.

What we collect

  • Your account: your first name and email address.
  • What you put in the app: your daily tasks, reminder times, habits, goals, streak and completed days.
  • Technical details: your time zone, your plan (trial or paid), your trial start date, and, if you turn reminders on, a push notification address that your phone or browser gives us so we can send them.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We receive your email, name, plan and payment status. We never see or store your card details.
  • Usage information: we use Google Analytics to see, in aggregate, which pages are visited and on what kind of device.

How we use it, and why

  • To run the app, save your data and sync it across your devices (to provide the service you signed up for).
  • To send sign-in emails and, if you turn them on, push reminders (to provide the service; push reminders only start when you choose to enable them).
  • To manage your free trial and subscription, and to answer your emails.
  • To keep the service secure and understand how it is used (our legitimate interests).

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We use trusted providers to run the service. They only handle data on our behalf:

  • Supabase – database and sign-in
  • Vercel – website hosting
  • Stripe – payments
  • Brevo – sends our sign-in emails
  • Google Analytics – usage statistics
  • cron-job.org – triggers our reminder checks (it receives no personal information)

Some of these providers are based outside the UK. Where that happens, we rely on recognised safeguards for international transfers.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account data. We keep payment records for as long as the law requires (usually six years).

Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or stop how we use it, or move it to another service. You can also withdraw consent for reminders at any time in your phone or browser settings. Email hello@tomorrowstartstonight.co.uk and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Security

You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email, and the database only lets each person see their own information. Data is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we take care to protect yours.

Cookies and storage

The app stores information on your device (local storage) to keep you signed in and remember your settings. Google Analytics may set cookies to measure usage. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the app will still work, though some statistics will be missed.

Children

Tomorrow Starts Tonight is not meant for anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, let you know.
